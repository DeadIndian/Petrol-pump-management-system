# BunkBook — Product

**BunkBook** is petrol pump management software for Indian fuel stations: daily sales and meter readings, credit customers and recoveries, purchases and stock, employees and payroll, expenses, banking, invoices, and 90+ reports — all on your own PostgreSQL database.

## Register
Product. Operators use it all day on desktop and phone, so clarity and density beat decoration.

## Users
Station owners, managers and accountants who need correct balances, fast data entry and reports they can trust.

## Principles
- Accuracy first: every sale, payment and reversal updates stock and account balances consistently.
- Fast entry: forms are dense, keyboard-friendly and forgiving.
- Fintech calm: deep teal on warm white, restrained accents, readable numbers.
- Own your data: runs locally, backups are first-class.
